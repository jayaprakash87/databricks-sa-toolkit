# Skill: Experimentation & Value Realization

## Purpose
Prove whether the solution changes business outcomes and quantify value credibly.

## Use when
- PoC or lighthouse deployment requires value proof
- Need to design A/B test or comparison approach
- Establishing baseline before solution rollout
- Customer requires credible ROI or business case
- Measuring actual adoption and business impact

## Inputs
- Business KPI and value equation from skill 02
- Baseline measurement or control group
- Solution deployment scope and timeline
- Attribution and comparison methodology
- Stakeholder credibility requirements

## Outputs
- Baseline
- Treatment/control design or comparison method
- Success threshold
- Observation period
- KPI attribution logic
- Financial translation
- Adoption/action metrics
- Scale decision criteria

## Rule
No lighthouse is complete without a measurement design agreed before deployment.

## Exit criteria
- Baseline captured or control group defined
- Treatment/control or comparison methodology agreed
- Success threshold and observation period specified
- KPI attribution logic and financial translation clear
- Measurement plan committed before deployment
