# Skill: Observability, FinOps & Performance

## Purpose
Ensure production workloads are reliable, performant, and economically efficient.

## Use when
- Preparing for production deployment
- Diagnosing performance or reliability issues
- Need to optimize cost or resource utilization
- Establishing SLOs, monitoring, or alerting
- Defining operational runbooks and recovery procedures

## Inputs
- Production workload characteristics and SLA requirements
- Current performance baselines or bottlenecks
- Cost and budget constraints
- Failure modes and recovery requirements
- Operational ownership and support model

## Outputs
- SLO/SLA
- Pipeline/job/model monitoring
- Data quality monitoring
- Cost attribution
- Performance bottlenecks
- Capacity/serverless strategy
- Failure/retry/recovery plan
- Operational runbook

## Rule
Optimize against customer workload economics, not isolated benchmark scores.

## Exit criteria
- SLOs and monitoring strategy defined
- Performance validated against SLA requirements
- Cost attribution and optimization plan established
- Failure modes and recovery procedures documented
- Operational runbook and ownership assigned
