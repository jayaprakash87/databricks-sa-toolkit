# Customer Meeting

## Goal
Lead a purposeful customer conversation with clear outcomes, persona-aware communication, and controlled pacing.

## Workflow
1. Identify meeting type and desired decision.
2. Identify personas, their priorities, and likely concerns.
3. Define what is known vs what must be validated.
4. Build an agenda around outcomes rather than products.
5. Prepare 5–8 high-value questions.
6. Decide what evidence, architecture, demo, or data is needed.
7. Plan likely interruptions and objections.
8. Reserve time to recap and agree next steps.

## Meeting leadership
- set a meeting contract
- validate assumptions
- summarize what was heard
- adapt depth by persona
- park rabbit holes consciously
- regain flow after interruptions
- close with decisions/actions

## Guardrails
- Do not turn every meeting into discovery.
- Do not use a fixed 45/60-minute script regardless of context.
- Do not narrate product screens when the audience needs business outcomes.
- Do not end without explicit next steps.

## Output
1. meeting objective
2. persona map
3. agenda
4. questions
5. key messages
6. evidence/demo plan
7. objections
8. closing/next step
