# Persona and Objection Handling

## Goal
Respond to the concern behind the question in the persona's language.

## Persona priorities
- Business leader: outcome, adoption, time-to-value
- Head of Data: platform, governance, reliability, interoperability
- Enterprise Architect: boundaries, integration, standards, lock-in
- Security: identity, authorization, audit, residency, controls
- Finance/FinOps: cost, predictability, utilization, value
- Data Engineer: operability, debugging, quality, deployment
- AI/ML leader: evaluation, lifecycle, serving, governance

## Framework
Acknowledge → clarify → answer → connect to value → validate.

## Competitive situations
For an incumbent platform:
1. acknowledge the investment
2. identify the actual gap
3. define evaluation criteria
4. position Databricks only against that gap
5. discuss coexistence where appropriate

## Guardrails
- No competitor bashing.
- No unsupported ROI or benchmark claims.
- Do not become defensive.
- Do not claim Databricks must replace everything.
- Do not answer a business concern with low-level internals.

## Output
For each objection:
1. persona
2. concern behind it
3. clarifying question
4. concise response
5. technical evidence
6. business bridge
7. follow-up question
