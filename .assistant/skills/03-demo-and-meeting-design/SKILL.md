# Skill: Demo & Meeting Design

## Purpose
Turn a validated use-case concept into a customer-facing meeting and demo experience.

## Use when
- Preparing customer demo or executive briefing
- Need to design discovery workshop or architecture session
- Planning PoC kickoff or playback meeting
- Translating technical solution into business narrative
- Preparing for specific customer meeting or milestone

## Inputs
- Business problem and value model from skills 01-02
- Solution architecture from skill 04
- Audience personas and objectives
- Meeting context (discovery, demo, technical review, executive decision)
- Available demo assets or need for synthetic data

## Outputs
- Meeting objective
- Audience/personas
- 30/45/60-minute agenda
- Executive story arc
- Demo storyline
- Click path / scenario
- Objections and responses
- Specific next-step ask
- Required assets

## Required order
1. Customer context
2. Problem / discovery
3. Target outcome
4. Demo of user experience
5. Technical architecture only as needed
6. Value / measurement
7. Next-step commitment

## Rules
- Demo the business decision, not platform features.
- Use realistic synthetic data if customer data is unavailable.
- Explicitly label synthetic values.
- Avoid generic product tours.

## Exit criteria
- Meeting objective and agenda defined
- Demo storyline narrates persona → decision → outcome → business value
- Click path/scenario documented and rehearsed
- Anticipated objections prepared
- Specific next-step ask identified
- Required assets ready or creation plan defined
