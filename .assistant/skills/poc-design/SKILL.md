# PoC Design

## Goal
Prove the smallest set of assumptions required for the customer to make the next decision.

## Workflow
1. Define the hypothesis.
2. Identify the uncertainty being reduced.
3. Narrow the scope to representative data/users/workflow.
4. Define out-of-scope explicitly.
5. Define business success criteria.
6. Define technical success criteria.
7. Define security/governance and cost criteria where relevant.
8. Identify dependencies and customer responsibilities.
9. Define milestones and evidence.
10. Define the decision at PoC completion.

## Guardrails
- A PoC is not a disguised full implementation.
- Do not choose success thresholds without customer agreement.
- Do not prove capabilities the customer already accepts.
- Do not omit operational or governance criteria when they are buying blockers.

## Output
1. hypothesis
2. scope/out-of-scope
3. users/data
4. success criteria
5. dependencies
6. risks
7. milestones
8. final decision
