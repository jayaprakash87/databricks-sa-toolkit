# Brief: Retail media clean room

A grocery chain sells retail-media placements to consumer brands. Brands demand campaign measurement — did their ads drive incremental sales? — but the grocer will not hand over loyalty-card transaction data, and brands will not share their full media-spend data. Legal on both sides prohibits raw data exchange.

The retail-media commercial director wants a measurement offering where brand and grocer data are joined for aggregate campaign analysis without either party seeing the other's row-level data. Auditability of what was computed is contractually required, and the grocer must be able to revoke a brand's access at campaign end.

Loyalty transaction data is already curated and governed in the grocer's lakehouse. Measurement is campaign-cadence (weekly), not real-time.
