# Brief: Executive KPI copilot

A European insurance group has a well-governed Unity Catalog estate: finance and claims KPIs live in curated gold tables with full column documentation, refreshed daily by established pipelines. Power BI dashboards exist but executives don't use them — they ask analysts ad-hoc questions by email, creating a 2–3 day answer cycle.

The CFO wants executives to self-serve answers like "why did the combined ratio move in motor this quarter?" in natural language, grounded strictly in governed numbers, on their phones, before board meetings.

Freshness of daily data is accepted as sufficient. The analyst team is worried about hallucinated numbers and insists every answer must be traceable to governed tables.
