# Brief: GenAI knowledge assistant for field technicians

An industrial equipment manufacturer has 9,000 pages of maintenance manuals, service bulletins, and safety procedures as PDFs in SharePoint. Field technicians call a central support desk for answers, averaging 40 minutes per resolution; wrong answers on safety procedures are a compliance risk.

The service director wants technicians to ask questions from a mobile device and get answers grounded in the current official documents, with citations, in under a minute. Documents are revised monthly; superseded versions must never be used in answers. Some bulletins are restricted by customer contract and must not be visible to all technicians.

There is no structured data component — the knowledge is entirely in documents. The company has no existing Databricks footprint for this use case and no demo data can include real customer bulletins.
