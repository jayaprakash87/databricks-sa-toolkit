# Brief: Predictive on-shelf availability

A grocery retailer loses an estimated 3% of revenue to on-shelf stock-outs. Store managers today walk aisles with paper gap-scan lists; replenishment decisions lag reality by hours. The retail director wants stores ranked every morning by predicted stock-out risk, with a prioritized task list per store.

POS transactions and inventory positions land in the retailer's lakehouse today, but the team is unsure how fresh the curated tables actually are — "somewhere between 15 minutes and a day" depending on the pipeline. Store operations run on a task-management app that field staff already use.

Success is measured as availability percentage uplift and reduced gap-scan labor. Leadership has been burned by a previous "AI transformation" that shipped dashboards nobody used.
