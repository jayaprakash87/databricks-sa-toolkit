# Brief: Regulatory claims data sharing

A property insurer must provide claims and exposure data to its national regulator quarterly, under a new solvency regime. Submissions must follow the regulator's schema, carry full lineage back to source, and be reproducible on demand for seven years. Late or incorrect submissions carry fines.

Claims data is already curated and governed in the insurer's lakehouse — the actuarial team certifies it quarterly today for internal solvency models. The regulator retrieves data via a secured exchange; it does not access insurer systems directly.

The head of regulatory reporting wants: correct schema mapping, an auditable handover process, revocation of access when submission windows close, and evidence for every figure submitted. Nothing else is in scope.
