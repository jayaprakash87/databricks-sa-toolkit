# Brief: Customer 360 for a retail bank

A retail bank has customer data fragmented across core banking, cards, mortgage servicing, and a CRM. Relationship managers see four different views of the same customer; contact-center agents put customers on hold to check other systems. The retail banking COO wants a unified customer view for relationship managers and branch staff, refreshed daily.

The project has visible executive sponsorship, and internal teams are already lobbying to attach initiatives: a churn model, a next-best-offer engine, and a "customer chatbot." The COO's stated scope is the unified daily view with correct entitlements — bankers must see only customers they are authorized for.

Source extracts exist for all four systems (nightly files). Data quality across systems is known to be inconsistent (duplicate customers, conflicting addresses).
